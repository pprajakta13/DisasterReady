About DisasterReady

DisasterReady is a Natural Disaster Preparedness Portal developed to help citizens stay prepared before, during and after disasters.

Mission

Provide disaster awareness, emergency assistance and preparedness resources.

🚨 Features

Live Alerts, SOS Requests, Resource Locator, Emergency History and Admin Dashboard.

Objective

Reduce disaster risks by providing quick information and emergency support.
